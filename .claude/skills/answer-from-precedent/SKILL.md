# Answering open questions from precedent

## Why this exists

Most questions the pipeline asks the owner have been answered before, on
another issue, in a contract, or in the constitution. The owner's time
should go to the ones that are genuinely new. There are three ways this
goes wrong:

- **Over-asking.** A settled question goes to the owner anyway. Spec 097
  SC-004 waited as an "older decision" although the live contract
  (`contracts/stop-point-record.md`) left the field out on purpose and the
  owner had merged #899 on it.
- **Over-answering.** A stretched precedent decides a new trade-off on the
  owner's behalf. This is the expensive direction: a reply from a local
  session posts under the owner's account, and on a lifecycle issue it
  starts a clarify run that folds the answer into the spec as if the owner
  had written it.
- **Losing the decision.** An answer given only in chat is invisible to
  the next session. #969's a/b/c choice was asked only in chat on
  2026-10-05, and a later session had to dig it out of a transcript.

## What counts as a precedent

From strongest to weakest:

1. **The owner's own acts and words**: comments the owner wrote (no
   "Generated by Claude Code" footer), the owner's replies to clarify
   questions, and merges and label changes the owner made (`merged_by`, the
   actor on a label event).
2. **Owner decisions recorded by a Claude session.** Much of the record is
   written by Claude: a session asks, the owner answers in chat, and the
   session carries on and writes the result down under the owner's account
   with the footer. Such a comment or line **counts** when it records a
   decision rather than proposes one:
   - it says so ("per the owner", "option 1 per the owner", "the owner
     chose", an `**Owner decision**` line); or
   - it answers a question the owner was asked; or
   - the owner acted on it afterwards (merged the PR it proposed, applied
     the label it asked for).

   Examples: #889's "fixed by #955, option 1 per the owner"; the "Owner
   decisions" list on the Roadmap (#890); #985's "the owner has chosen the
   approach: sync at the source".

   When an answer rests on a Claude-written record alone, say so in the
   citation ("recorded by Claude on #N, 2026-10-05"), so the owner can catch
   a decision that was written down wrong.

   **Doesn't count:** a Claude-written recommendation, proposal or triage
   the owner never answered or acted on, or a research agent's summary.
   Check a summary against its source before relying on it. A research
   agent once reported that a container run had passed, when #966 had in
   fact been closed by a host-mode pass.
3. **The constitution** (`.specify/memory/constitution.md`; its amendment
   history is in `constitution-history.md`) and **CLAUDE.md**.
4. **Live contracts and merged code.** Live contracts are under
   `specs/*/contracts/`; what the code does today is a decision somebody
   merged. A merged spec's `spec.md`/`plan.md` is a historical record: it
   shows what was decided then, and a live contract or the code wins where
   they differ.
5. **`research.md` D-entries of merged specs.** They hold the reasoning
   behind a trade-off, which is what "same shape" is judged on.
6. **Closed issues and PRs on the topic.** A parked spec's open questions
   (#890 "Parked") are questions, not answers.

**The precedent has to match the shape:** the same kind of decision, the
same trade-off, the same constraints. A precedent about one mechanism
doesn't decide another's policy. If you find yourself arguing that it
matches, it doesn't; raise it.

**Always the owner's, whatever the precedent:**

- new credentials, permissions, App scopes or repository variables;
- model tier or spend (constitution II);
- any constitution amendment;
- merging a spec PR, a plan PR or an amendment (constitution V);
- promoting a `spec-proposal` to `spec-request` (constitution X);
- parking or closing a lifecycle;
- widening a spec beyond what was asked;
- anything the owner said they want to decide.

## Procedure

**0. Collect the open questions.** Look in each of these sources:

- **Lifecycle issues in clarify.** These carry a `spec:*` label and
  `stage:spec` or `stage:clarify`. Look for the bot's latest "Action
  needed: Answer the open clarification questions" comment with no owner
  reply after it.
- **The Roadmap's open lists.** The open `disposition:tracking` issue
  titled "Roadmap" (#890): its "Proposed" and "Owner decisions" lists.
- **The Board status issue's owner list.** The open `disposition:tracking`
  issue titled "Board status": what it says waits on the owner.
- **The Maintenance backlog's owner lines.** Lines on #889 that end "for
  the owner" or start with "Decide".
- **Whatever the owner named in the request.**

**1. Search, strongest source first.** For example:

```
R=$(git remote get-url origin | sed -E 's#(\.git)?/?$##; s#.*[/:]([^/:]+/[^/:]+)$#\1#')
gh search issues --repo "$R" "<topic words>" --limit 30
gh search prs    --repo "$R" "<topic words>" --limit 30
gh api "repos/$R/issues/N/comments" --paginate \
  --jq '.[] | {who: .user.login, at: .created_at, claude: (.body | test("Generated (by|with) \\[Claude Code\\]")), body: .body[0:600]}'
grep -rn -i "<topic>" CLAUDE.md .specify/memory/ specs/*/contracts/ specs/*/research.md
```

**2. Decide each question.** Each one comes out as one of two things:

- **Settled.** Give the answer, the precedent (a link, plus the sentence
  that decides it, quoted), and what to do. For a clarify question that
  comes with an options table, the answer is an option letter plus one line
  of why.
- **Needs the owner.** Give the question in one sentence, 2–3 options with
  their implications, and your recommendation with its reason. Also name
  the nearest precedent you rejected and why it doesn't fit; that is often
  what the owner needs to see.

**3. Act on what is settled.**

- **Clarify questions.** Any owner-account comment on such an issue starts
  a clarify run. `wing-commander-2-clarify.yml` gates on a `spec:*` label,
  `stage:spec` or `stage:clarify`, and an OWNER, MEMBER or COLLABORATOR
  author (or the requester), and a local session's comments post as the
  owner. So post **one** reply per issue, covering every question, and
  only once every question on it is settled or answered by the owner. If
  any question needs the owner, hold the whole reply, raise that question,
  and post once they answer, marking which answers are theirs. Write each
  answer as `Q2: B. <why> (precedent: #N / path:line)`.
- **Other decisions** (Roadmap items, backlog lines, PR threads). Act on a
  settled item only when the owner asked you to work through decisions;
  otherwise report it.
- **Raising a question.** Ask in chat, one decision per question, with the
  recommended option first and marked "(Recommended)". Use
  `AskUserQuestion` when it is available.

**4. Write down what the owner decides, where the next session will
look.** When the owner answers in chat, record it on the issue or PR it
governs:

- start the comment with `**Owner decision (YYYY-MM-DD):**`;
- give the question, the chosen option, and that it was decided in chat;
- `gh search issues --repo "$R" "Owner decision"` then finds it.

Two exceptions:

- On an issue in clarify, the consolidated reply is the record. A second
  comment would start a second clarify run.
- A roadmap-level decision goes in the Roadmap's "Owner decisions" list.

## Watching for questions

When asked to watch for questions, run this skill on a schedule: a `/loop`,
or a trigger that fires back into the session. Write the trigger's prompt
as "run the answer-from-precedent skill on #N, #M", not as a restatement of
these rules, so the rules have one home. If nothing is new, do nothing and
say nothing. Stop the watch once every watched lifecycle has left clarify.

## Reporting

- **Settled:** each item with its answer, its precedent and what you did.
- **Needs you:** each item with the question, its options and your
  recommendation.
- **Skipped:** anything not a question for this skill, with why.

Lead with whatever needs the owner.
